HOW IT WORKS

Small, specific, and you stay in charge.

  1. We talk it through

    Book a consultation and tell me what's eating your time. If it isn't something I can fix — or isn't worth fixing — I'll tell you that instead of selling you something.

  2. I scope it in writing

    What I'll build, what it costs, and what it will and won't do. No surprises later.

  3. I build it around how you work

    Using the tools you already have where possible. You see it working before anything goes live.

  4. It runs, and I watch it

    You're billed once it's confirmed working in your business — not before. If you want out, you keep what's yours and we're done.
