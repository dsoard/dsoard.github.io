HOW IT WORKS

Advice you can act on, and you stay in charge.

  1. We talk it through

    Book a consultation and tell me what's eating your time. If it isn't worth changing, I'll tell you that instead of selling you something.

  2. I look at how it really happens

    Who does what, in which tools, and where it stalls. The way the work actually moves, not the way it's supposed to.

  3. You get clear recommendations

    What to change, what to leave alone, and which tools fit — starting with the ones you already have. In plain language, in writing.

  4. You decide what happens next

    Do it yourself, hand it to your team, or bring in someone to set it up. The recommendations are yours to keep either way.
